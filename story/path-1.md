by: Jayden Cruz

## path-1

The village is five crooked houses and a bakery. Every window is dark except the bakery's, where warm light spills onto the cobblestones.

A little sign on the house next door says KEEPER in faded paint.

You have arrived to take over the lighthouse, but the tower is dark and a ship is expected to arrive before sunrise.

[Knock on the bakery door](#bakery)

[Knock on the keeper's house](#old-keeper)

[Go back to the crossroads](#crossroads)

## bakery

Rosa the baker opens the door in a cloud of flour.

"You're the new keeper? Then you'll want this. The tower stairs are black as a boot at night."

She hands you an old brass lantern.

get: lantern

"There's pie, if you'd like some," she adds. "Blackberry."

[Stay for a slice of pie](#pie)

[Thank her and head out](#old-keeper)

## pie

The pie is warm, and the chair by the oven is soft. You close your eyes for just a moment.

When you open them, it is morning. The ship made it past the rocks by pure luck. The harbor master is waiting outside with a very long list of complaints.

THE END

## old-keeper

An old man opens the door just a crack.

"Abernathy," he says. "I kept that light for forty years."

"The tower door is locked, and the only key went down with my boat when she sank off the south beach. If you want that light lit, the key is in the wreck."

[Travel to the south beach](#south-beach)

[Return to the village](#path-1)

## south-beach

The waves roll across the sand. Pieces of an old wooden boat are scattered along the shore.

You spot the remains of Abernathy's boat farther out in the shallow water.

[Search the wreck](#shipwreck)

[Return to the village](#path-1)

## shipwreck

You climb carefully through the broken remains of the boat.

Under a loose floorboard, you discover a small iron key.

get: iron key

You have found the key to the lighthouse!

[Take the key to the lighthouse](#lighthouse)

[Return to the village](#path-1)

## lighthouse

The lighthouse stands above the cliffs. Its great lamp room is dark, and the thick oak entrance door is locked.

[Unlock the lighthouse door](#lamp-room) if iron key

[Return to the south beach](#south-beach)

## lamp-room

You unlock the door and climb the spiral staircase.

At the top, you find the great lamp surrounded by glass prisms. You light the wick with your lantern.

The light blooms across the water. Far out in the fog, a ship's horn answers with two long blasts.

The ship has found its way safely past the rocks.

You have saved the village.

THE END

## ending-dark

You never find the key or reach the lighthouse in time.

The ship disappears into the fog, and the village wakes to terrible news.

The lighthouse remains dark.

THE END
